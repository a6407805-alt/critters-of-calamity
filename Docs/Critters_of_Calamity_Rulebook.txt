Objective:
Critters of Calamity is a four player asymmetrical board game where four players play unique factions with their own buildings, abilities, and units on the same hexagonal gameboard. To win the game, you must compete against the other players gathering resources, relics, and utilize your faction’s unique strengths to be the first to accumulate 30 Authority.

1st Draw phase:
Draw 1 card from the relic deck and add it to your hand.

Faction Craft: Craft one of your faction’s building, token, card, equipment, or tile you can then place it on a legal tile. You cannot place buildings or tokens on river tiles.

3rd Main Phase:
During your main phase, you can take up to three of the main phase actions below. You can choose any combination of main phase actions to take including using multiple main phase actions to move, battle, loot, or recruit multiple times.

Possible Main Phase Actions: 
Move: move any number of your units on the same tile by 1 tile.
(Note: units must always be on tiles and they cannot be between tiles.)
Battle: initiate a battle between enemy units, special units, buildings, or tokens. There is an explanation of how to battle in Part 11 and a battle example in Part 13.
Loot: The gameboard has three different tiles. Forest tiles which are green, ruin tiles which are gray, river tiles which are light blue. Any of these tiles that has at least one of your factions’ units and that has no enemy pieces, may be “looted.” When you loot:

On a river tile: roll a 6-sided dice if the roll result is odd, gain 1 Stone from the resource deck, if even receive 1 Gold from the resource deck and add it to your resource pile. 
On a ruin tile, draw the top card of the relic deck.
Each tile can only be looted once per turn.
(Note: 20% of the cards in the resource deck are Gold meaning if you loot a forest tile you have a 20% of getting Gold. Looting a river or ruin tile grants a 50% chance of receiving Gold. Different factions want wood, stone, and gold in varying amounts.)

Part 9 - Unit Stats Unit Stats: 
All Units and Special Units have 3 stats: attack, defense, and hitpoints (HP). A unit's stats are written on their unit or special unit card as Attack/Defense/HP. For example, the Special Unit Curly Tail’s stats are 3/2/2 which means he has 3 Attack, 2 Defense, and 2HP. 

All basic units have 1HP. Special Units often have multiple hitpoints. This means that many Special Units must be successfully battled multiple times before they die. Equipment, abilities, and crafted relic deck cards can alter a unit's attack, defense, or HP.

Part 11 - How to Battle: During your main phase you can use a main phase action to battle if both your faction's units and another faction’s units are on the same tile. When you battle, follow the battle phases in order.
Phase 1 - Attack Declaration:When you battle, start by declaring an attack against one faction whose pieces are on the tile as any of your units.Each battle can only target one faction at a time. If multiple faction’s units are on the same tile you must choose one faction to attack. The other non-participating faction plays no part in the battle.The turn player declaring the attack is the Attacker, the player being attacked is the Defender. The attacker chooses which of their units on the battling tile will participate in the battle. The defender must include all of their units that are on that tile in the battle.(Note: Buildings and Tokens are not involved in battles between faction units. For rules on how to destroy buildings and tokens see Part 12 of the rulebook.)Phase 2 - Dice Roll:After the attacker declares their attack and chooses which of their units will participate in the battle, both the attacker and the defender roll 2 six-sided dice and record their individual rolls, this is your dice roll.

Phase 3 - Attack and Defense Points:
Then the attacker totals up the attack points of all of their attacking units that are participating in that battle, this total is the attack point score.
(Note: each unit has attack/defense/HP listed on its unit or special unit card, the attack number is each unit's attack points.)

The defender totals up the defense points of all their units on the battling tile, this total is the defense point score.
(Note: each unit has attack/defense/HP listed on its unit or special unit card, the defense number is each unit's defense points.)

Then the attacker adds their dice roll and their attack point score for the Attacker’s Total. 
The defender adds their dice roll to their defense point score for the Defender’s Total. 

The player with the higher total is the winner. The player with the lower total is the loser.(Note: this is the winning the battle that Curly Tail’s Momentum meter references.)If the Attacker’s Total and Defender’s Total are equal: both factions take 1HP damage that they can distribute however they wish among their participating units. The battle then ends immediately with no further HP damage being dealt.

Phase 4 - Damage Calculation:If  Attacker’s Total and Defender’s Total are not equal, the winner deals damage to the loser through the Winner’s Damage Dealt formula.Winner’s Damage Dealt Formula:
Damage Dealt = Winner’s Attack Point Score minus the Loser’s Defense Point Score.
(Note: if the damage dealt goes negative, the winner still deals 1HP damage.) 

Damage Cap: Per battle, each unit can only receive 1HP damage. The winner's calculated damage dealt cannot exceed the number of the winner's units that participated in the battle. Meaning that 3 units can only deal up to 3 HP damage. If the total HP damage dealt exceeds the number of available units, ignore the excess.

Mandatory Loser Damage: The loser always deals 1HP damage back to the winner (unless they are the Horde of the Millions whose species trait Oblivious to Danger makes them unable to deal mandatory loser damage).  

Both the attacker and the defender choose how to distribute HP damage among their participating units.

(Note: many special units have multiple HP, this means they will need to successfully battle multiple times to die.)

Tracking Unit Damage: To track damage on a Special Unit keep a dice onto the unit which is equivalent to their current HP. For example, since Curly Tail has 2 HP, put a dice on “2” on Curly Tail’s Special Unit Card, when he takes 1HP, change the dice side to “1” to represent each mark of damage.

All damage is applied simultaneously at the end of the damage calculation phase.

Phase 5 - Attack Resolution:
All units reduced to 0HP are removed from the board and are placed into that faction’s unit supply. 

This is the end of the battle, if the turn player has any remaining main phases attacks they can choose to declare another battle, use a legal ability, make another main phase action, or proceed to the end phase.

If battling is still unclear, Part 13 of the rulebook provides a full example of a battle.

Battle Winner: High Combined Total.
Battle Loser: Lower Combined Total.
If Equal Attacker’s Total and Defender’s Total: Both factions take 1HP and the battle ends.

Part 12 - How to Destroy Tokens and Buildings:
Tokens and Buildings cannot  be battled while any of their faction units are on the same tile (with the exception of the Chrysalis which can participate in a battle between units). If tokens and/or buildings are on a tile without any of their faction’s units the turn player can spend a battle action to destroy tokens and/or buildings on that tile equal to the number of attacking units on that tile even if the attacking units have 0 attack. Buildings and tokens do not have attack, defense, or HP meaning that buildings and tokens cannot deal damage or apply mandatory loser damage. 

Each destroyed token or building grants the attacker 1 Authority.


Part 13 - Full Battle Example:
The following example walks through a complete battle, phase by phase, using two of the game's factions.
Groundwork: The turn player is playing Curly Tail the Tyrannical's Warband. They spend one of their three main phase actions to declare an attack against the Horde of the Millions player, who has 3 Millions units on the same tile as the Warband. On this tile there are three Squirrel units but they do not participate in the battle as they are from another faction.
Phase 1 - Attack Declaration:The Warband player is the Attacker and the Millions player is the Defender. As the attacker, the Warband chooses which of their units on the tile join the battle: they include Curly Tail (3/2/2) and 2 Warband Units (1/1/1 each). As the defender, the Millions player must include all 3 of their Millions units (0/0/1 each).
Phase 2 - Dice Roll:Both players roll 2 six-sided dice. The Warband player rolls a 3 and a 5 for a dice roll of 8. The Millions player rolls a 2 and a 4 for a dice roll of 6.
Phase 3 - Attack and Defense Points:The attacker totals their attacking units' attack points: 3 attack (Curly Tail) + 1 attack (Warband Unit) + 1 attack (Warband Unit) = an Attack Point Score of 5.The defender totals their units' defense points: 0 defense (Millions Unit) + 0 defense (Millions Unit) + 0 defense (Millions Unit) = a Defense Point Score of 0.
Then each player adds their dice roll to their score:
Attacker's Total: 8 + 5 = 13
Defender's Total: 6 + 0 = 6
13 is higher than 6, so the Warband player is the winner and the Millions player is the loser. 
(Note: This is the won battle Curly Tail's Momentum meter references so the Momentum meter increases by 1.)
Phase 4 - Damage Calculation:The winner deals damage using the damage dealt formula: Winner's Attack Point Score minus Loser's Defense Point Score = 5 − 0 = 5 HP of raw damage.
Damage Cap: The damage dealt cannot exceed the number of the winner's units that participated, the Warband had 3 units, so the cap is 3 HP. The raw 5 HP damage is reduced to 3.
Mandatory Loser Damage: The Million’s species trait Oblivious to Danger makes them unable to deal mandatory loser damage. Every other faction deals 1HP mandatory loser damage to the winner.
Now each player distributes the damage they've taken among their own participating units, with each unit able to take at most 1 HP:
The Millions Player takes 3 HP. With 3 units available, they must assign 1 HP to each, all three Millions are marked for removal.
All damage is applied simultaneously.
Phase 5 - Attack Resolution:All units at 0 HP are removed and returned to their faction's supply. The Millions lose all 3 Millions units.
After the battle: Because the Warband won, Curly Tail's Pillager trait triggers a free Pillage, looting the battle tile for 1 random resource from the resource deck drawing a Gold resource card, which does not count as the tile's once-per-turn loot (all Millions died here, so the tile is now clear regardless). The Warband has now used one of its three main phase actions and may declare another battle, take another main phase action, use a legal ability, or move to its end phase. The Curly Tail player chooses to use the recruit action to summon 1 Warband unit at both of their War Parties.

Unique Faction Tile - Fire Tile: 
Fire Tiles are hexagonal overlay tiles placed on top of forest tiles. Only Tar Pit Tokens may be placed on a Fire Tile, no other building, token, or tile can be placed on a Fire Tile. Tar Pit Tokens cannot be destroyed by Fire Tiles.

End-Phase Resolution: 
During your end phase, resolve your Fire Tiles one at a time. On each Fire Tile, take these steps in order:
Clear Squirrels: Return all of your Squirrel units on this Fire Tile to your supply. Gain 1 Charcoal card to your resource pile for each one removed. 
Burn structures: Remove 1 building or token of your choice on this Fire Tile. Then roll two dice, if you roll doubles, destroy every building and token on every Fire Tile on the board (Tar Pit Tokens cannot be destroyed by Fire Tiles). 
Drive out enemies: Each enemy unit on this Fire Tile is moved by its controller to an adjacent tile of their choice. A unit moved onto a forest tile takes 1HP; a unit moved onto a river or ruin tile takes none. 
Spread: Roll one die. If you roll a 1 or 6, place a new Fire Tile on a forest tile holding one of the enemies you just drove out, and deal 1HP to every unit on that new Fire Tile (Spread is not considered entering a fire tile, as the units are not moving onto a fire tile). 
Burn the Deck: Burn the top card of the resource deck. If it is Gold, gain 1 Authority.

Rules That Apply For Fire Tiles Throughout the End Phase:
End-phase damage cap: No unit can take more than 1HP total from your end-phase Fire Tile resolution (steps 1-5) during a single end phase, no matter how many Fire Tiles or steps would damage it. 

A unit can only be driven out (step 3) once per end phase. If it is later forced back onto a Fire Tile, it still takes the 1HP (if on a forest tile, and if it has not already reached the cap) but is not moved again. 

Burned cards go to the burned area and cannot return to any deck or pile except through the relic deck card Marble Aqueduct.

Other Fire Tiles Effects:
Entering a Fire Tile (In-Movement Damage):
Whenever an enemy unit moves onto a Fire Tile during any player's turn, 1 of the moving player’s units on that tile takes 1HP.
(Note: a unit driven onto an existing Fire Tile in step 3 counts as entering it. A unit that a new Fire Tile is placed beneath in step 4 does not.)
The entering a fire tile damage is dealt once each time a unit enters a Fire Tile. It is separate from the end-phase resolution above and is not limited by the end-phase damage cap meaning a unit can take 1HP entering a Fire Tile on its own turn and a further 1HP from your end-phase resolution. 

Removing a Fire Tile:
If another faction has a unit on a Fire Tile, during their main phase they may discard 2 Stone from their resource pile to remove that Fire Tile from the field and gain 1 Authority.

Building - Wallow: Once during your excavation phase, you can discard 1 Wood and 1 Stone from your resource pile to place 1 Wallow on a River tile with a Capybara unit or on a Forest Tile that is adjacent to a river tile with a Capybara unit on it. When you recruit, summon 1 Capybara unit at each Wallow.

Species Trait - Watering Hole: An enemy unit becomes a Guest in any of these cases:
It enters a tile with a Wallow and its controller chooses not to attack on that tile. 
The Capybara builds a Wallow on a tile that already has an enemy unit(s) on it. 
When a player purchases a card from the Capybara trader.
The Riverkeeper is on a tile with enemy unit(s) on it. 
While a unit is a Guest, each player that controls a Guest unit draws 1 card from the resource deck during their end phase. A unit loses Guest status when it moves out of, or is moved out of, the tile with the Wallow (or, for case 4, the tile with the Riverkeeper).

Species Trait - Selfless Nature: When a guest would take damage in battle, a Capybara unit on that tile absorbs 1HP damage from that battle. 

Species Trait - Customary Greeting: Once during your end phase, gain 1 Authority for each player who has a guest unit (cap 2 Authority per turn). 

Species Trait - Exchange Rate: Once during your main phase, you can discard three of the same type of resource card to add 2 of the same type of resource card from the resource deck to your resource pile. 

Unit - Capybara:
Unit Stats: 1/1/2
Capybara Ability - Forage: Once during your main phase, when a Capybara is on a river tile , you can take 1 Gold or 1 Stone from the resource deck and add it to your resource pile.

Capybara Ability - Semiaquatic: For 1 movement action, a Capybara unit(s) can move 2 River Tiles.

Special Unit - The Riverkeeper:
Unit Stats: 1/4/3
Summoning Condition: During your main phase when there are 2 or more Wallows on the board, you can discard any 2 resource cards and 1 Gold from your resource pile to summon the Riverkeeper on a tile with a Wallow.

Riverkeeper Ability - Hospitality: Once during your main phase, if you have a guest on the Riverkeeper's tile the guest may draw 1 relic deck card; when they do, the Capybara player gains 1 Authority and adds 1 card from the discard pile to their hand

Riverkeeper Ability - Immovable: The Riverkeeper cannot be moved by an enemy effect but they can be moved by a tile effect.

Riverkeeper Ability - Commerce: While the Riverkeeper is on the board, guests can purchase Traded Good Cards as if they were on a tile with the Capybara Trader.

Special Unit - Capybara Trader:
Unit Stats: 0/2/2
Summoning Condition: If the Capybara Trader is not on the board during your excavation phase, you can discard 2 relic deck cards from your hand to place the Capybara Trader at a Wallow.

Capybara Trader Ability - Traveling Caravan: During any player’s main phase when they have a unit on the tile with the Capybara Trader or their unit is on an adjacent tile, they can purchase 1 of the Trader’s faceup Traded Goods cards from the stall by paying the resource cost to the Capybara player, then the purchasing player gains 1 Authority, the Capybara player gains 1 Authority and the purchasing player places 1 of their basic units from their supply onto a tile with a wallow as a guest. During each endphase, refill the stall to 3 cards.

Capybara Trader Ability - Peddling: The Capybara Trader receives 1 free movement action during each of your turns, during each movement action the Capybara trader can move up to 2 land tiles or up to 3 river tiles.

Faction Deck - Traded Goods Cards:
When you purchase these cards from the Capybara trader, place the purchased card faceup in front of you. You can use the effect of the card during an appropriate time and then discard the card.
Rusty Shovel: The next time you use the looting action, you can draw 3 cards from the resource deck and end the looting action, then discard this card. Cost: 3 resource cards.
Equipment - Clay Edict: You can equip this card to 1 of your special units; it gains + 1 tile movement for the next three turns. Cost: 3 Resource cards.
Prayer Beads: During your excavation phase, you can draw the top 2 cards from the relic deck, then discard this card. Cost: 3 Resources.
Silver Spoon: During your excavation phase, you can add 2 relic cards from the discard pile, then both you and the Capybara Player gain 1 Authority. Cost 2 Resources.
Copper Figurine: After a dice has been rolled, you can discard this card to re-roll 1 dice (can be used during the battle dice roll phase). Cost: 3 Resources.
Broken Sickle: Add 1 relic card from the discard pile to your hand, then discard this card. Cost: 1 Resources.
Horse Hair Brush: Your next unearth, assemble, or Disassemble action costs 1 less resource of your choosing, then discard this card. Cost: 2 Resources.
Old Jug: During your main phase you can heal a special unit for 1HP, then discard this card. Cost: 2 Resources.

Ways Enemies Can Score  Authority From You:

Killing the Riverkeeper gives 2 Authority.

Faction 11 - The Briared Flower:
Description: There is a legend that in the farthest corner of the Continent, a flower once sought to grow so high it could touch the sun. But the higher it climbed, the more attention it drew from those who coveted its beautiful petals, and slowly, those petals metamorphosed into fiery thorns. When at last the flower was cut down, it bloomed one final time in an act of defiance. Now one of its seedlings has flowered, born with an instinctual hatred of other creatures. It hurls its spines across the Continent so that it might be left alone and its seedlings might take root, and when it is stripped bare, it thrashes out one last fury to protect what it has sown. Bloom violently, spend yourself utterly, and rise again from your own scattered seed. Claim Authority through everything you destroy.

Setup: Place the Blooming Briar with 6 Thorns, 2 Thorn Seeds, and 1 Prickly Thicket on a forest tile with no enemy pieces within 4 tiles other factions cannot place their pieces within 4 tiles of any Briared Flower pieces.

Building - Prickly Thicket: Once during your excavation phase you can discard 2 Wood to place 1 Prickly Thicket on a forest tile with a Thorned Seed or Blooming Briar. When you recruit, place 1 Thorned Seed at each Prickly Thicket.
Effect: Once during your main phase, If the Blooming Briar is on a tile with a Prickly Thicket you can remove the Prickly Thicket and give the Blooming Briar 1 Thorn.

Species Trait - In Half Bloom: During your main phase, you can remove 2 resources from your resource pile and 3 Thorned Seeds from the board to place 1 Blooming Briar with 2 thorns on a tile with a Prickly Thicket. 

Species Trait - Branching Out: Thistle Barrage gains +1 tile range for each Thorned Seed on the tile with the Blooming Briar (cap +2 tile range).

Species Trait - Rooted In Place: During your turn, the Blooming Briar can only move 1 tile for the entire turn.

Species Trait - Pollination: During your end phase, gain 1 Authority if you have Thorned Seeds on 4 or more different tiles (gain 2 Authority if there are 7 or more Thorned seeds on different tiles).

Species Trait - Instinctual Hatred: The Blooming Briar player cannot trade with other players. 

Unit - Thorned Seed:
Unit Stats: 0/0/1
Thorned Seed Ability - Bursting Into Bloom: During your main phase, you can remove up to 3 Thorned Seeds from the board, deal 1HP damage to 1 unit on each tile where a Thorned Seeds was removed, then gain 1 Authority if you killed an enemy unit.

Special Unit - Blooming Briar:Unit Stats: 6/1/1
Blooming Briar Trait  - Thorned Body:
The Blooming Briar carries Thorns (0-6). While the Blooming Briar has 1 or more Thorns you can activate the following three abilities
Thistle Barrage: Up to twice during your main phase, If enemy units are within 4 tiles of the Blooming Briar you can target any number of enemy units or special unit on that tile, then spend 1 Thorn and 1 resource from your resource pile for each targeted unit or special unit on that tile to deal 1HP damage to each. Then place 1 Thorned Seed per discarded thorn on an adjacent forest tile to the tile with the targeted unit(s) or special unit(s). Alternatively, you can spend 1 Thorn to destroy all enemy buildings and tokens on a tile within 3 tiles of the Blooming Briar if that faction’s units or special units are not on that tile. Gain 1 Authority for every 2HP damage dealt by Thistle Barrage.
(Note: This does not initiate a battle.)
Thorn Armor: When the Blooming Briar would take HP damage, you can remove 1 Thorn per 1HP damage instead (this follows the unit damage cap).
Sun Eater: Once per turn during your main phase, you can remove 2 Thorned Seeds on the tile with the Blooming Briar or its adjacent tiles and 3 resources from your resource pile to gain 2 thorns. 

Blooming Briar Ability - Frenzied Bloom: When the Blooming Briar is at 0 Thorns gain the following three abilities:
Ability - Withered Blossom: Gain +3 attack. The Blooming Briar can deal 2HP damage to a single unit bypassing the unit damage cap limit.
Ability - Furious Lashing: Once during your main phase, deal 1HP damage to all enemy units within 3 tiles. Gain 1 Authority for every 2HP damage dealt by this ability.
Ability - Propagation: When the Blooming Briar is removed from the board, one of the enemy units on that tile where the Blooming Briar was removed takes 1HP damage, then you place 1 Thorned Seed on an adjacent forest tile, and the killer gains 2 Authority.

Ways Enemies Can Score  Authority:
Destroying a Prickly Thicket gives 1 Authority.
Killing the Blooming Briar gives 2 Authority.
